Our story

From one windowsill to helping beginners keep plants happy everywhere.

Why we started

Most first-time plant owners lose a plant within a month. We believed the problem was advice, not people. So every plant we sell comes with a simple care card, and every order includes a message you can reply to.

We source from small nurseries, pot our plants in reusable containers and ship in recycled packaging.

What we care about

Beginner friendly

We label every plant by care level so you pick one you can keep alive.

Sustainable packaging

Recycled boxes, paper fill and pots you can reuse.

Honest advice

If a plant will struggle in your room, we will tell you before you buy.
