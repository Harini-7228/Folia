General watering guide

Quick rules of thumb to keep your indoor and outdoor foliage thriving in any season.

☀️

Hot & Sunny

Water in the evening. Keep plants out of harsh afternoon sun and mist leaves.

🌧️

Rainy Days

Skip watering. Ensure pots have drainage holes so roots don't sit in water.

❄️

Cold & Cool

Water less frequently. Keep plants away from cold windows and drafts.
